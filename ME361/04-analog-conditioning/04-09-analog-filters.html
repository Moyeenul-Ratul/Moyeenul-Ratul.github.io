Clean a 5 Hz signal buried under hum and hiss

Filter benchinputfiltered outputclean signal aloneM(f)

Sine input, Butterworth, fc ≈ 20 Hz, k = 1: hum survives at about 37 %. Raise k to 4: hum drops to about 3 % and hiss disappears, but the output now lags the clean signal by about 21 ms. Now choose the square-wave input with fc ≈ 60 Hz and k = 6: the Butterworth output rings after every edge and overshoots by roughly 30 %. Switch to Bessel with the same k: the edges are softer but clean, with almost no overshoot, because every harmonic is delayed by nearly the same time. High-pass at 2 Hz removes nothing useful here; push it to 20 Hz and the 5 Hz signal itself disappears.
