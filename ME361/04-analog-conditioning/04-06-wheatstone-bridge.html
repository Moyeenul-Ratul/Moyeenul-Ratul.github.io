Quarter bridge at +25 % and 10 V: the exact output is 0.556 V while the linear rule says 0.625 V, a 12 % gap, and R₁ dissipates about 0.25 W. Switch to the half bridge: the gap vanishes. Choose the dummy-gauge option: equal changes in adjacent arms give zero output.
