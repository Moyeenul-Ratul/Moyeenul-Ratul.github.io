Each track is read by its own light source and detector (dots). An orange segment reflects light and reads 1. The misalignment slider skews the row of detectors: the inner (MSB) detector sits ahead of the line by the set angle, the outer (LSB) detector behind it. The right plot shows the decoded reading (Gray readings are converted back to binary first) for every shaft angle.

With straight binary and 6° misalignment, look at the right plot: tall orange spikes appear at sector boundaries, for example a reading of 3 near 360° where the truth is 7 or 0. Slide the angle to 356° and read the error in the readout. Now switch to Gray code: the spikes vanish and the only error left is a reading one sector early or late within a few degrees of each boundary (near 360° that shows as 0 instead of 7, which is still the neighbouring sector). Set misalignment to 0 and both codes are perfect, but no real encoder is perfectly aligned.
