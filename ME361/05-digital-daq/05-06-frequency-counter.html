Simulator · gated counting

Watch the counter fill during the gate, and see the ±1 count

Frequency counter with a 4-bit ripple counter showninput and gatecounted pulsescounter bits

The counter adds one at each rising edge that passes the AND gate. The four green rows are its first four flip-flops: \(Q_0\) toggles on every counted pulse, \(Q_1\) at half that rate, and so on, so their pattern is the running count in binary. The readout shows the full count, the frequency \(N/T_g\) and its error.

With 10.3 Hz and a 1 s gate, move the gate-start slider: the count flips between 10 and 11, so the meter reads 10 or 11 Hz although the input never changes. That is the ±1 count. Set \(T_g\) = 2 s: the count is 20 or 21, read as 10.0 or 10.5 Hz, so the resolution has doubled at the cost of a slower update. Press Run the gate to watch the counter fill.
