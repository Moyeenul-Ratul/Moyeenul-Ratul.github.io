Keep f = 10 Hz and drag fs slowly down from 100 Hz. Above 20 Hz the blue curve sits on the grey one. Below 20 Hz they part: on the right panel the black dot moves onto a falling (red) leg, the blue wave is inverted, and its frequency fs − 10 keeps dropping as you lower fs. At fs = 12 Hz you get the 2 Hz alias; at fs = 10 Hz every sample lands on the same point of the cycle and the sine looks like a constant. In the right panel the zigzag is the folding diagram straightened out: rising legs (green) are in phase, falling legs (red) are inverted.
